About us

Who we are and how the money is used.

Our mission

Charity Events is a not-for-profit community initiative. Our mission is to make it effortless for people to find, trust and join charity events in their own city, and to make sure that every dollar raised reaches the cause it was given for.

We publish each event with its full description, its fundraising goal and the amount raised so far, so you can see exactly what your support achieves. Organisations that do not meet our fundraising policy are suspended and removed from the listings.
